> HOW PROJECTS ARE BUILT

Every project starts with a simple idea. The goal is to turn that idea into something that can actually be built, tested and improved.


I first focus on understanding the basic structure, then I write the code, test the interface, find problems and debug them.

The objective is not simply to finish a project. The objective is to learn something from building it.
